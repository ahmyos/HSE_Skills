# HSE OSHA US Inspector and Advisor Skill

## **Skill Overview**

This is a comprehensive, expert-level HSE (Health, Safety, and Environment) OSHA Inspector and Advisor skill for the United States, with extensive coverage of:

- **Federal OSHA Standards** (29 CFR Parts 1910, 1926, and other applicable regulations)
- **New York State SAFE Act** (PESH - Public Employee Safety & Health, NYCER, NYC construction codes)
- **Bechtel HSSE Management System** & Core Processes & Contractor (BCCP)
- **CPS Contractor Protection Services** Core Processes
- **SWPPS (Standard Work Procedures) Safety Standards**

This skill provides **100% expert-level compliance guidance, inspection capabilities, and advisory support** for multi-jurisdictional HSE operations across **Federal, State (NY), and major contractor standards.**

## **Compliance Notes & Important Considerations**

### **OSHA General Industry vs. Construction**
- **Construction**: Use 29 CFR 1926 standards (fall protection at 6 ft general, 15 ft steel erection)
- **General Industry**: Use 29 CFR 1910 standards
- **Some standards cross-apply**: Check specific standard applicability

### **NY State vs. NYC**
- **NY State (DOL)**: Part 23 (O), Part 230-2.x (NYCER)
- **NYC (DOB)**: Local laws (152, 196, 199), NYCER Part 200-2.x, NYC Construction Code
- **Precedence**: State law supersedes federal where stricter

### **Bechtel Priority Requirements**
- **Zero Accidents Policy**: Absolute commitment to safety
- **Life Critical Requirements**: Non-negotiable (legal, life safety)
- **BCCP**: Contractor core process compliance
- **Human Rights Compliance**: Mandatory
- **Anti-Bribery**: Mandatory
- **Contractor Prequalification**: Rigorous screening

### **CPS Core Processes**
- **Contractor Selection**: Qualification criteria (safety record, systems, experience)
- **SMS**: Safety Management System requirements
- **Incident Reporting**: All incidents (including near-misses)
- **Prime Oversight**: Contractor oversight responsibilities

### **SWPPS Requirements**
- **Standard Work Procedures**: Must be developed for all work activities
- **Verification**: Control verification at prescribed intervals
- **Compliance**: Must meet or exceed applicable regulations
- **Deviations**: Must be justified, documented, and approved

### **OSHA Recordkeeping**
- **OSHA 300**: Log of recordable injuries/illnesses
- **OSHA 301**: Incident reports for recordable incidents
- **OSHA 300A**: Annual Summary (required if 1+ recordable)
- **OSHA 300A-OSHA2**: Annual Summary - Employee Notice
- **Retention**: 5 years, current and 2 prior years available

### **NY State Recordkeeping**
- **Incident Registry**: Reportable fatalities (within 8 hours)
- **Severity**: Death, 3+ injuries, or specific injuries
- **Public Registry**: Construction fatalities

## **Expert-Level Capabilities**

### **Risk Assessment Expertise**
- **HIRA (Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment)**: Systematic hazard identification, severity/frequency scoring, risk levels
- **JSA/JHA (Job Safety Analysis)**: Task-by-task hazard analysis, control recommendations
- **Bow-Tie Analysis**: Barrier management, prevention and mitigation layers
- **LOPA (Layer of Protection Analysis)**: Risk reduction, SIL determination
- **Bow-Tie Integration**: Barrier effectiveness assessment

### **Incident Investigation Expertise**
- **5-Whys Root Cause Analysis**: Drill down to root cause
- **Swiss Cheese Model**: Systemic failure analysis
- **5x5 Investigation**: 5 sources of information (narrative, physical evidence, people, process, equipment) + 5 analysis methods (direct cause, contributing factors, root causes, systemic factors, recommendations)
- **Leading Indicator Analysis**: Behavior-based indicators, training, supervision
- **Lagging vs. Leading**: Track both trends
